My Approach

Four principles.
One system.

Everything I do is built around sustainable progress - not quick fixes. Here is how we get there together.

01

Start where you are

We begin with your current level, your goals, and what feels realistic. Good training should feel clear, effective, and sustainable from the start.

02

Build strength step by step

Progress matters, but it doesn't need to be extreme. The goal is steady improvement that helps you feel stronger, fitter, and more confident over time.

03

Train for real life

Your program should work with your schedule, energy, and responsibilities - not against them. The best plan is the one you can actually keep.

04

Focus on results that last

I'm not interested in quick fixes. I coach for long-term progress, better habits, and a stronger body you can rely on.

FAQ

Common questions.

Do you have experience with clients?

I'm launching my coaching practice and taking on founding clients. My 10+ years of personal training, two injury rehabilitations, and cell biology background mean every program I write is grounded in real experience - just not commercially listed yet.

What does the free consultation involve?

30 minutes on video or in person. We talk about your goals, I assess what's realistic, and I explain exactly what working together would look like. No sales pitch at the end - just a plan.

I'm based outside Prague - can we work together?

Yes. Online coaching includes full programming, video check-ins, and WhatsApp support. I work with clients across time zones.

What if I have an injury or have never trained before?

That's exactly who I work best with. I started from injury myself. We begin with movement quality, not weight on the bar.

How does the founding price work?

Founding clients get 50% off the regular session rate, locked in for their first month of training. This rate exists because I'm building my practice and want my first clients to benefit from early access — you get a certified, experienced trainer at a significantly reduced price while I grow my client base. Only 10 spots are available at this rate. Once they're filled, the price returns to standard. No tricks — just a straightforward deal for people who take a chance on me early.

Do you offer nutrition coaching separately?

Yes - see the Nutrition tab in Services. This is separate from personal training and can be combined or standalone.

Where do we train?

This will be discussed during your consultation. I have several options ranging from public gyms in the Prague city centre to private studio spaces - depending on your preferences and goals.

Do I need gym equipment for online training?

No — your program is built around whatever you have access to. Whether that's a full commercial gym, a set of dumbbells at home, or bodyweight only, the programming adapts to you. We figure out your setup during the consultation and go from there.

Is the free consultation really free?

Yes, completely. It's a 30-minute video call — no credit card, no commitment, no sales pressure at the end. We talk about your goals, I tell you honestly what I think will work, and you decide if you want to move forward. If it's not a good fit, I'll say so.

About Me

Built on hard work.

●

I've been training for over 10 years, shaped by both performance-focused lifting and a long-term interest in how the body adapts, recovers, and improves over time.

After a knee injury and two surgeries, my approach shifted. Training became less about intensity for its own sake and more about longevity, precision, and intelligent progression.

That perspective is reinforced by my background in cell biology, which influences how I think about training at a systems level - adaptation, recovery, and consistency always matter more than extremes.

I am an ISSA Certified Personal Trainer (2026), and I apply that foundation to structured, evidence-based coaching tailored to the individual.

Every program I design is built around your body, your schedule, and your goals - with a focus on sustainable progress and clean execution over time.

No templates. No noise. Just focused coaching that moves you forward consistently.
